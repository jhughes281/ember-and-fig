Shop Candles: Hand-Poured in Houston, Shipped Worldwide

Ember & Fig is a small candle studio in Houston, one of the most international cities in the country. We pour coconut-soy wax with cotton wicks and phthalate-free fragrance oils, and every scent starts with a real place in town: a sweet shop, a market, a porch, a stretch of bayou.

Two-wick candles burn for roughly 50 hours, XL three-wicks for about 80. Trim the wick to a quarter inch before each burn and let the first burn reach the edge of the glass so the wax pools evenly. When the candle is done, freeze the glass overnight, pop out the last of the wax and keep the vessel.

We ship from Houston to the U.S., Canada, Mexico, the U.K. and the E.U. Prices are shown in your chosen currency as an estimate and charged in U.S. dollars at checkout.
